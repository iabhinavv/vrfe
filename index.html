What You'll Learn in the Foundations Course

A detailed breakdown of the core concepts covered in our most popular course.

Basics of Market

  • Understand how markets work (buyers/sellers, price discovery, liquidity)
  • Learn key market terms (trend, volatility, volume, market sentiment)
  • Know different market participants and what moves prices
  • Build a strong foundation for technical + fundamental analysis

Technical Analysis

  • Learn the purpose of technical analysis and when to use it
  • Understand support/resistance, trend direction, and momentum
  • Get a repeatable way to read price + volume (instead of “guessing”)
  • Start building a simple chart-reading workflow

Types of charts

  • Know the differences between line, bar, and candlestick charts
  • Learn what each chart type is best for (clarity vs detail)
  • Understand timeframes and how perspective changes across them
  • Read common candlestick components (open/high/low/close)

Reversal Patterns (Part 1)

  • Identify early signs of potential trend reversal
  • Learn high-probability reversal structures and how they form
  • Understand common failure points (false reversals) and how to avoid them
  • Practice basic confirmation ideas (levels, volume, follow-through)

Reversal Patterns (Part 2)

  • Expand your reversal pattern toolkit (more variations + context)
  • Learn how to combine patterns with support/resistance and trend context
  • Improve timing by focusing on confirmation and invalidation levels
  • Develop discipline around risk management for reversal setups

Continuation Patterns

  • Spot continuation patterns that signal trend strength (not trend change)
  • Understand consolidation vs breakout behavior
  • Learn how to plan entries, targets, and invalidation for continuation setups
  • Reduce chasing by waiting for structure + confirmation

Fundamental Analysis

  • Understand what fundamental analysis measures (value, growth, stability)
  • Learn core drivers that affect price over time (earnings, macro, sector)
  • Know key metrics and what they indicate (profitability, leverage, cash flow)
  • Build a simple checklist to evaluate a company/asset before investing
